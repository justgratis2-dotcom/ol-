This Cookie Policy explains how ebice.site uses cookies and similar technologies.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work efficiently and to provide information to site owners.

2. Cookies We Use

TypePurposeSet By
EssentialRemember your cookie consent choiceebice.site (localStorage)

3. Google AdSense Cookies

Google uses cookies to serve ads on this site. These cookies allow Google and its partners to serve ads based on your visits to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or www.aboutads.info.

4. Managing Cookies

You can control and delete cookies through your browser settings. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of this and other websites. You can also clear the consent choice stored by this site by clearing your browser's localStorage.

5. Consent

When you first visit our site, we ask for your consent to use non-essential cookies. You may reject non-essential cookies, and the site will still function, though ads will be less relevant.
