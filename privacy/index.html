Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

This policy explains what information Ebice (ebice.site) collects when you visit the site, how it is used, and the choices you have. It applies together with our Terms of Service.

1. Who we are

Ebice is operated by Ebice, located in Spain. For privacy matters, you can contact us at contact@ebice.site. For the purposes of the GDPR, the operator of the site is the data controller.

2. Your code stays on your device

The obfuscation tool runs entirely in your browser. The Lua code you paste or load is processed locally and is not uploaded to our servers by the tool. We do not see, store or share your scripts or the obfuscated output.

The page writes nothing to storage on its own: no cookies, no localStorage, no analytics, no crash reporting, and no request to any server of ours. The content-security policy forbids the page from submitting a form or loading a plug-in, and it carries no form at all.

Two comment lines can be added to the file you download or copy, both of them switches you control under “Output & privacy”:

  • Signed header — the name and version of the tool, the list of active layers and a timestamp. It contains nothing about you.
  • Ownership stamp — a short one-way fingerprint of your source text, so you can later show that a given output was produced from a given file. It is not a signature, not encryption, and no copy of it is kept anywhere.
Closing or refreshing the page clears your code — “Wipe on close” is on by default, and “Your code never leaves this tab”. Keep your own copy of your original source.

4. Cookies and advertising

Ebice is funded by advertising served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies, mobile identifiers and similar technologies to show ads based on your previous visits to this and other websites, and to measure ad performance.

  • Where required by law (the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland), personalized advertising is only used after you consent through the consent message shown on this site. That message comes from a consent management platform certified by Google and integrated with the IAB Transparency and Consent Framework, so the consent record is kept by Google and by you in your own browser, not by us.
  • You can change your choice at any time through the same consent message, through the advertising settings links below, or by clearing this site's cookies.
  • You can opt out of personalized ads in Google Ad Settings or via aboutads.info and youronlinechoices.eu.
  • To learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Most browsers also let you block or delete cookies. If you do, some ads or site features may not work as expected.

5. Legal bases (GDPR)

  • Consent for advertising cookies and personalized ads.
  • Legitimate interests for keeping the site secure, preventing abuse and keeping server logs.
  • Legitimate interests / your request when you contact us and we reply.

You can withdraw consent at any time. Withdrawing does not affect processing done before.

6. Sharing and international transfers

We do not sell your personal data. We share technical data only with service providers needed to run the site (hosting) and with advertising partners such as Google. Some of these providers are located outside your country, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or Standard Contractual Clauses.

We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the site and its users.

7. How long we keep data

Server logs are kept only as long as needed for security and operations, typically a few weeks. Emails you send us are kept while we handle your request and for a reasonable time afterwards. Advertising partners keep data according to their own policies.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • access the personal data we hold about you;
  • correct it, or ask us to delete it;
  • restrict or object to certain processing;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time.

To use these rights, email contact@ebice.site. If you are in the EU or EEA and believe we handled your data unlawfully, you can complain to your data protection authority. In Spain this is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (AEPD).

California residents can ask what personal information is shared for advertising and opt out of its "sale" or "sharing". We do not sell personal information for money; advertising partners may receive identifiers as described above.

9. Children

Ebice is a tool for scripters and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. In countries with a higher age of digital consent, such as 14 in Spain or 16 in some EU countries, anyone below that age needs a parent or guardian's permission to use the site. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

10. Security

We keep the data we handle to a minimum: there is no account, no database and nothing to breach on our side. On the page itself we run a content-security policy that blocks <base> hijacking, plug-ins and form submissions, we refuse to render the page inside another site's frame, we discard advertising code that does not belong to the site owner, and the pages that host the tool build their interface without injecting any of your text as markup.

The site also checks the domain it is being served from. That check exists to stop copies of the tool being run elsewhere; it never inspects your files, your code or anything else on your machine, and it reports nothing to us.

No online service is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security, and a determined person with a browser can always read what a page sends to their own device. Do not put secrets in a script that you expect obfuscation to protect.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows the latest version. If we make significant changes, we will make that clear on the site.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Write to contact@ebice.site.
